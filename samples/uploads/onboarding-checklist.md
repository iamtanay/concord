# New Hire Onboarding Checklist

## 1 Before day one

The hiring manager prepares a welcome plan for the first two weeks. IT ships the laptop to the new hire's home address.

## 2 First week

Each new hire is paired with an onboarding buddy from their team. The buddy meets the new hire every day of the first week.

## 3 First month

New hires complete the security awareness course during their first month. The hiring manager holds a check-in at the end of the first month.
