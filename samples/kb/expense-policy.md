# Travel and Expense Policy

## 1 Travel

Employees book economy class for flights shorter than six hours. Business class is permitted for flights longer than six hours with director approval.

## 2 Meals

The daily meal allowance while travelling is $75. Alcohol is not reimbursable.

## 3 Submission

Expense reports must be submitted within 30 days of the expense. Receipts are required for any single expense above $25.

## 4 Approval

Expenses above $5,000 require approval from the Chief Financial Officer.
