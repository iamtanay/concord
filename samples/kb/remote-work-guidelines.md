# Remote Work Guidelines

## 1 Eligibility

All full-time employees may work remotely up to three days per week. Customer support staff follow the team rota instead.

## 2 Equipment

Northwind provides a laptop and a monitor to every remote employee. Company laptops use full-disk encryption.

## 3 Working hours

Core collaboration hours are 10:00 to 15:00 in the employee's local time zone. Meetings outside core hours are optional.

## 4 Security

Company data must not be stored on personal devices. Public Wi-Fi may only be used with the company VPN enabled.
