# Incident Response Plan

## 1 Severity levels

A severity 1 incident is a full outage of the customer platform or a confirmed data breach. A severity 2 incident is a partial outage affecting some customers.

## 2 Response

The on-call engineer acknowledges a severity 1 page within 15 minutes. An incident commander is appointed for every severity 1 incident.

## 3 Notification

Affected customers are notified of a confirmed data breach within 72 hours. The Data Protection Officer notifies the regulator.

## 4 Review

A written post-incident review is published within five business days of resolution. Reviews are blameless.
