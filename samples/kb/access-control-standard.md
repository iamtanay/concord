# Access Control Standard

## 1 Principles

Access to production systems is role-based. Every employee is granted the minimum access needed for their role.

## 2 Authentication

Multi-factor authentication is required for all employees on every system. Passwords must be at least 14 characters long.

## 3 Reviews

Access rights are reviewed every quarter by each team manager. Accounts of departing employees are disabled within 24 hours of their last working day.

## 4 Privileged access

Administrator access to production databases requires approval from two engineering leads. Privileged sessions are recorded and retained for audit.
